# GRC policies

## Sharing the SOC 2 report
The SOC 2 Type II report may be shared only after the prospect signs an NDA, and only through the Trust Center portal (trust.example.com), never as an emailed PDF. Pre-answered SIG Lite responses are also in the Trust Center.

## Vendor onboarding requirements
Vendors that will handle Restricted data need a SOC 2 Type II report (Type I is not sufficient), a DPA, a BAA if PHI is involved, and a recent penetration test summary. Vendors handling only Internal data need a security questionnaire.

## Contractors and third parties
Contractors who need customer data work inside our VDI environment and may not export data. They need a DPA, and a BAA if PHI is involved, before any access.
